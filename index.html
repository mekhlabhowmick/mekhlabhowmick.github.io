Mekhla Bhowmick

PhD Scholar in Economics · University of Calcutta

I am a final-year PhD scholar in Economics at the University of Calcutta, where I am completing my doctoral thesis, Rethinking Domestic Labour: A Theoretical Inquiry, under the supervision of Prof. Anjan Chakrabarti.

I examine domestic labour through a class-focused, Marxian lens, studying how class, gender, and power shape household work and its relationship to the wider economy. Alongside this theoretical work, I have undertaken qualitative research through primary surveys on domestic labour and domestic production in Indian households.

My broader interests bring feminist economics and the political economy of the household into conversation with questions of social reproduction, informal production, and the history of economic thought.

Research interests

  • Political economy of domestic labour
  • Marxian class analysis
  • Feminist economics
  • Social reproduction
  • Informal production in India
  • Home-based enterprises
  • History of economic thought
