Mekhla Bhowmick

PhD Scholar in Economics, University of Calcutta

I am a final-year PhD scholar in Economics at the University of Calcutta, where I am writing my doctoral thesis, Rethinking Domestic Labour: A Theoretical Inquiry, under the supervision of Prof. Anjan Chakrabarti.

My research rethinks domestic labour through a class-focused, Marxian lens. I ask who performs domestic labour, where it is performed, and how it connects to the wider economy, gender and power, and processes of social reproduction. Alongside my theoretical work, I have also undertaken empirical research in the context of Indian households.

More broadly, I am interested in feminist economics, the political economy of the household, informal production, and the history of economic thought.
