Research

My doctoral thesis, Rethinking Domestic Labour: A Theoretical Inquiry, re-theorises domestic labour through a class-focused approach where class is understood in terms of organization of surplus labour. I examine the class organisation of domestic labour in households along with its conditions of existence and explore how different forms of domestic production can constitute the domestic sphere.

Further, the study empirically investigates the outsourcing of domestic work to waged domestic workers, the organization of domestic and care work through domestic staffing agencies, and the production of commodities through home-based enterprises, both capitalist and non-capitalist. These empirical investigations serve to substantiate the theoretical argument that the organization of domestic labour in such varied kinds of domestic production point to possibilities of transition within Indian households.

    Title translated from the original Bengali article Charjar Darshan: Karl Marxer Theses on Feuerbach
    Saros Publishers, Kolkata · 2026 · ISBN 9788199253308
    Aryaman Roy, Deepro Majumder, Kaustav Saha, Mekhla Bhowmick, Prithwiraj Saha, and Sayonee Majumdar
    Authors are listed in alphabetical orders of their first names. Equal contribution
    Summary

    In 1845, Marx articulated the initial framework of The German Ideology in the form of eleven “Theses on Feuerbach.” This book presents a Bengali translation of the Theses on Feuerbach, rendered with reference to both the German and English texts, accompanied by a discussion of each thesis.

    In the twenty-first century, at a time when conventional left-wing politics faces uncertainty regarding political philosophy, Marx’s “Philosophy of Praxis” offers a framework for thinking about a different kind of transformative politics.
